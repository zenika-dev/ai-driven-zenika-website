## PROBLEM STATEMENT
Prospective clients, candidates, partners and training buyers who visit Zenika's current website find an outdated site that does not clearly explain the Conseil, Ingénierie and Formation offer. As a result, visitors leave without understanding what Zenika does and without getting in touch.

## TARGET AUDIENCE
Prospective clients and training buyers want to know what Zenika offers and who it has worked for. Candidates and partners want to understand Zenika's scale and approach.

## KEY MESSAGES
- Zenika is a technology partner close to its clients ("partenaire technologique de proximité").
- 20+ years of experience: 600+ people, 12 agencies, 3 continents.
- From decision to delivery: Conseil & CxO Advisory, Engineering & Delivery, Formation & Adoption.
- Zenika turns information-system complexity into measurable business value.
- Leading organisations already work with Zenika.

## USER STORIES
- As a prospective client, I want to see Zenika's three offers so that I can tell whether Zenika fits my need.
- As a prospective client, I want to see Zenika's clients so that I can trust its experience.
- As a training buyer, I want to find the Formation & Adoption offer so that I know Zenika can upskill my teams.
- As a candidate or partner, I want to see Zenika's size and approach so that I understand who Zenika is.
- As any visitor, I want a clear way to contact Zenika so that I can start a conversation.
