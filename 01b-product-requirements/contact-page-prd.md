## PROBLEM STATEMENT
Prospective clients, candidates, partners and training buyers who visit Zenika's current website find an outdated site that does not make it clear how to start a conversation with Zenika. As a result, interested visitors leave without getting in touch.

## TARGET AUDIENCE
Prospective clients and training buyers want to raise a question or a project with Zenika. Candidates and partners want a direct way to reach Zenika.

## KEY MESSAGES
- Your next challenge starts here ("Votre prochain défi commence ici").
- A question, a project or a need to transform your organisation are all welcome.
- One address reaches Zenika: info@zenika.com.

## USER STORIES
- As a prospective client, I want to email Zenika in one click so that I can describe my project.
- As a training buyer, I want to know where to send my request so that I reach the right people.
- As a candidate or partner, I want Zenika's contact address so that I can start a conversation.

## PAGE CONTENT
- Parlons-nous: "06 / Parlons-nous" label, heading "Votre prochain défi commence ici.", one-line intro — Zenika marketing — draft in reference mockup.
- Contact action: mailto button to info@zenika.com, with the address also shown as text — Zenika marketing — exists (address confirmed).

## CALLS TO ACTION
- Primary: "Écrivez-nous" button opens mailto:info@zenika.com.
- Secondary: None

## LOCALIZATION
French is the default and is written first; English translations follow, written and approved by Zenika marketing. Singapore localisation is undecided.

## SUCCESS METRICS
- Lighthouse mobile score of 95 or above for performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO.
- Zero axe WCAG 2.2 AA violations in both themes and both locales.
- Zenika marketing signs off the page against the reference mockup.

## OUT OF SCOPE
- A contact form: the mockup's form is replaced by a mailto button, and a form needs a separate decision.
- Agency addresses, phone numbers or maps.
